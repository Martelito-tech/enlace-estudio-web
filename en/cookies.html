COOKIES · LAST UPDATED 03/10/2026

Cookie policy.

This website doesn't use cookies: not its own, not third-party ones, not for analytics and not for advertising. Nor does it store anything in your browser by other means, such as local storage. That's why you don't see a cookie banner: there's nothing to accept or reject.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser to recognise you on later visits, remember your preferences or measure what you do. Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE) requires websites to ask for your permission before using any cookies that aren't strictly necessary.

What this website loads

Everything you see comes from our own server: the text, the images and also the fonts, which we host ourselves instead of requesting them from Google Fonts. When you open the website, your browser doesn't contact any third party.

When you click a link to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube or LinkedIn, you leave this website, and those platforms apply their own cookies and policies.

How to check

You don't have to take our word for it. In most desktop browsers, press F12, open the Application (or Storage) tab and look at the Cookies section for enlaceestudio.com: it's empty.

If this changes

If we ever add something that uses cookies or stores data in your browser, such as visitor statistics, we'll update this page before doing so and, where the law requires it, we'll ask for your permission before switching it on.
